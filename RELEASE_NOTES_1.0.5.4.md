# FIREOUT SHINE 1.0.5.4

**Seasonal FESTIVAL (FH6-style), automatic UTC rotation.**
- Seasons rotate automatically for every player on a shared UTC clock: 7-day weeks aligned to UTC Mondays (same pattern as COKA). Spring Sakura → Summer Night Races → Autumn Drifts → Winter Run.
- Each season has a race, a photo challenge and a drive-to, with yen or paint rewards that match the existing economy.
- A live pop-up feed shows the current season, a countdown to the next season, and event cards. The FESTIVAL phone app lists every event as AVAILABLE or DONE, with a progress bar and countdown.
- New events can be added from GitHub without rebuilding the game (`events.json` or `seasons/events.json` on main). The game caches the feed offline and falls back to built-in events.
- Light seasonal world look: sakura petals, warm night dust, autumn leaves, snow, plus a soft tint. No bloom or glow.
- Settings → Force season (DEV) is a hidden testing override and is OFF by default. Normal play always follows the automatic UTC season.

**EX SCREEN RECORDER 2026.** On the pause menu itself: START/STOP RECORDING, live REC status, and System sound on/off (off by default, no microphone). Finished MP4s save to your Downloads folder. F9 also starts/stops. No admin.

**Full HD across the board.** Default window 1920x1080. City textures (asphalt, sidewalks, facades, shopfronts, env) are 2048px. EX HD and RTX both run the full HD cinematic pipeline (16x aniso, 256 px cube-map, long LOD). Higher building detail (floor ledges, parapets, more roof props) and cinematic materials on city surfaces. Skyscrapers use real-life Tokyo massing: multi-setback crowns, mechanical floors, needle antennas, slim glass towers and a denser Japanese metro skyline (not a generic western CBD). Characters (Ana, Daniel and friends) and cars keep their existing look — not cel-shaded. The sky dome uses a flat cel band look for a cleaner cinematic horizon. No bloom.

**Cinematic city look.** Richer sun and street lighting, contact AO on hard surfaces, darker asphalt with brighter lane markings, warmer sidewalks, longer draw distance and atmospheric haze, stronger car contact shadows and clearcoat. Characters (Ana, Daniel and friends) are unchanged. Still no bloom or glow.

**Circular speedometer.** While driving, a clean ring gauge wraps the speed number (km/h in the centre, nitro bar underneath). Flat colours only — no glow or bloom.
