Taxi driver voice (assets/voice/taxi_en_*.wav): pre-rendered offline with Piper TTS (https://github.com/rhasspy/piper)
using the en_GB "northern_english_male" (medium) voice, trained on the OpenSLR 83 dataset
(http://www.openslr.org/83/), CC BY-SA 4.0 International. The rendered clips are shared under the same licence.

"Mei" released by the MMDAgent Project Team (http://www.mmdagent.jp/), Creative Commons Attribution 3.0.
