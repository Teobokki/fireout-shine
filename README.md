# ファイアアウト・シャイン — FIREOUT SHINE

Neon street racing and police chases through **Shinko Ward**, an original night-time Tokyo, with **ANA**, **DANIEL** and **MIYOYAZU**.

## Features (v1.0.4)
- New playable character MIYOYAZU, a construction worker in a hi-vis vest and hard hat, with two abilities (v1.0.4)
- BUILD: type anything (pagoda, house, skyscraper, tower, bridge, stairs, castle, cat, car, rocket...) and MIYOYAZU builds it in front of her in the same style as the city. Builds are solid and you can walk on them, with UNDO and CLEAR ALL (v1.0.4)
- TSUNAMI: a SMALL, MEDIUM or BIG wave floods HIKARI BEACH and Tokyo. Cars float, everyone swims, and STOP TSUNAMI drains it (v1.0.4)
- IX LAPTOPOS G9 laptop (LAPTOP button or L) with BANK, MAP, SHOP, MUSIC, EMAIL, PHOTOS, the IX NAVI BROWSER (an in-game web) and MISTO 2 snaps with face lenses, streaks and stories (v1.0.4)
- CLOTHING and FOOD in the pause menu, and animated eating with 12 foods and drinks, each with its own 3D prop (v1.0.4)
- NPCs speak Japanese with the English translation underneath, using a full-coverage Japanese font (v1.0.4)
- JP TAXI: see-through glass, an Indian driver with a British English voice, first-person back-seat view (press C for the rear view), and a reckless, cheerfully unbothered driving style (v1.0.4)
- Dense traffic with traffic signals at every junction, plus a TRAFFIC DENSITY setting; no more bloom or glow, for natural lighting (v1.0.4)
- Open neon city: scramble crossing with giant video screens, sign-covered streets, elevated expressway, the original 新光タワー lattice tower, power lines, lanterns, shopfronts; HIKARI BEACH with boardwalk, pier and palms
- Day/night cycle (24 real minutes = 24 h): blue daytime sky with drifting cumulus clouds, sun and horizon haze, golden hour, then neon night; lock Day or Night in Settings
- Slim modern glass skyscrapers with mullions, setbacks and crowns; turquoise shallows at the beach
- ADD MONEY in the pause menu: type an amount or use +1K / +100K / +1M / +1B / +10B / MAX; total cash is capped at ¥100,000,000,000 (v1.0.2)
- The EX IX G9 phone sits left of the speedometer and driving buttons while you drive (v1.0.2)
- ANA and DANIEL's arms hang naturally, with real five-digit hands, and move: they swing when you walk and run, hold weapons in hand, grip the wheel and scrub in the shower (v1.0.3)
- Walk freely around ANA & DANIEL'S HOUSE: every spot (shower, wardrobe, bed, laptop save, TV, fridge, front door) shows an [E] button when you are near it (v1.0.3)
- DANIEL's modern short haircut, plus smooth walking on HIKARI BEACH sand, the boardwalk, the pier and over kerbs at any frame rate (v1.0.3)
- Six districts with names in English and Japanese, tinted on the minimap and full map, with a popup when you enter one
- Play as ANA, DANIEL or MIYOYAZU and switch any time (Q, or click a headshot); your partner follows on foot and rides with you
- Take any car (F): the driver jumps out and runs off. 10 original car designs
- Street races with AI rivals (city sprint, tower circuit, coastal run), a time trial, a beach drift zone, speed cameras, neon tokens
- 指名手配される GET WANTED: police chases with heat stars: break line of sight to escape, or get BUSTED
- FRGARAGE: paint colours and custom plates (up to 8 characters), saved per car
- EX IX G9 phone: Emergency Services (ambulance) and JP TAXI with destination picker and skip-trip
- CAMERA app on the phone: selfies with a pose (ANA or DANIEL), SELMOTES faces (smile / neutral / goofy frown), a rear camera, and photos saved to Pictures\FIREOUT SHINE (v1.0.3)
- Weapon wheel (hold Tab): fists, baseball bat, pickaxe, taser, pistol, SMG, shotgun; KAZAN SUPPLY ammo refill. Non-gory: people get knocked down and flee
- Stable arcade driving model (no flipping, sub-stepped collisions, auto-unstick and R reset)
- Voiced English + Japanese street chatter (offline TTS) with subtitles; duo banter
- On-screen buttons for every control (can be hidden in Settings), keyboard, mouse and gamepad

## Controls
W/S accelerate/brake · A/D steer · Space handbrake · Shift nitro/run · F enter/exit car · Q switch character · T phone · E start event · Tab weapon wheel · RMB aim · LMB attack · C camera (taxi: first person / rear view) · L laptop · R reset car · H horn · M map · B build (MIYOYAZU) · N tsunami / stop tsunami · Esc menu
