FIREOUT SHINE v1.0.4.1 (patch for v1.0.4)

- ANA now has BUILD and TSUNAMI. They work exactly as they did in v1.0.4, with the same buttons and keys.
  - BUILD (B, or the BUILD button): type anything and ANA builds it in front of her in the same style as the city. That includes pagodas, houses, skyscrapers, towers, bridges, walkable stairs, castles, shops, animal statues including the new horse, and vehicles. Misspellings are matched to the closest word, and unknown words get their own invented building. UNDO and CLEAR ALL work as before.
  - TSUNAMI (N, or the TSUNAMI button): SMALL, MEDIUM or BIG, or a severity slider, with the 津波警報 TSUNAMI WARNING banner. STOP TSUNAMI drains the water and puts everything back to normal.
  - ANA keeps her own look and outfits, and the build animation plays on her.
  - DANIEL does not have BUILD or TSUNAMI, so their buttons are hidden while you play as him. Anyone can still press STOP TSUNAMI.
- MIYOYAZU has been removed. The crew is ANA and DANIEL again: the character switch, Q, headshots, profile, CLOTHING, phone contacts, laptop mail, pedestrian lines and help text are all updated.
  - Saves from v1.0.4 where MIYOYAZU was active load as ANA, with DANIEL as her partner, and nothing else is lost.
- ANA's skin is more realistic, closer to how MIYOYAZU's looked. The tone is warmer and richer while keeping ANA's own colour family, the pores are calmer, and the shading is smoother with no sparkly speckle. Her face, arms, hands and legs all match, in every outfit. There is still no glow or bloom.
