ファイアアウト・シャイン FIREOUT SHINE v1.0.3

- FIXED: ANA always had her hands in her pockets. Her model was sculpted with the hands tucked into the trouser pockets, and the arms were part of the body mesh, so they could never move. Both arms now hang relaxed at her sides with the hands visible. They are separate shoulder and elbow joints, so they swing while you walk, pump bent when you run, grip the steering wheel when you drive, and rest on the lap as a passenger. This applies to every outfit (default, crop top, bikini, police).
- Real hands: ANA and DANIEL now have a palm, four fingers and a thumb on each hand, in every outfit, instead of the old mitten shape. The fingers are relaxed and slightly curled at the sides, close around a weapon when you hold it, make a fist when you punch, wrap the steering wheel when driving, and stay open while scrubbing in the shower.
- DANIEL had the same pockets pose and gets the same fix: hands out, swinging arms, and his T-shirt sleeves move with his arms.
- Weapons now sit in the right hand. Guns are held lowered at your side and raised two-handed when you aim (RMB). The bat and pickaxe swing with the arm, and fists throw a real punch.
- DANIEL's haircut: the long ends are trimmed. The sideburn locks that hung past his ears and the hair over the back of his neck are now a short, tapered modern cut. Same colour and the same textured style on top. His character-switch headshot was re-rendered to match.
- Shower: ANA (and DANIEL) now actively scrub in a loop. Both hands lather the hair, then each hand scrubs the neck and shoulders, then the arms, with the hands alternating. The swimsuit, shower mist and camera framing are unchanged.
- HIKARI BEACH and sidewalks: walking is smooth at normal speed. When the frame rate dropped below 20 fps, each frame's movement was capped, so ANA and DANIEL walked in slow motion (half speed at 10 fps). The game now sub-steps those frames, so walking and running stay at full speed. Feet also follow the real ground height: the boardwalk, the pier deck, the sloping sand and dunes, and the kerbs. You step up and down kerbs smoothly instead of popping or walking through the boardwalk or pier.
- ANA & DANIEL'S HOUSE is now free roam. The old button menu is gone: you walk around inside in third person with the same controls as outside (WASD or arrows, Shift to run, Q to switch). The living room, kitchen, bedroom and shower corner have solid walls and furniture, a doorway into the bedroom, and a follow camera that stays inside the room and never clips through walls. Walk up to something and a button appears ([E] SHOWER, [E] WARDROBE, [E] SLEEP, [E] SAVE GAME, [E] WATCH TV, [E] EAT SNACK, [E] LEAVE HOME). Press E or Enter, or click it. Sleep, save, the wardrobe and the shower scrub work as before, and Esc still leaves the house.
- New CAMERA app on both phones (ANA's blue EX IX G9 and DANIEL's green one), next to Contacts and JP TAXI. It opens in SELFIE mode: the camera swings round in front of your character's face at arm's length, a little above. They hold the phone out in one hand and wave with the other, and your partner steps in behind to photobomb. A/D changes the angle and W/S the tilt. FLIP (R) switches to the normal rear camera. The big round TAKE PHOTO shutter at the bottom (click it, or press Space or Enter) saves a PNG without the HUD to Pictures\FIREOUT SHINE (for example selfie_20261022_143001.png or photo_...png), with a white flash and a "Saved" message. If the Pictures folder can't be used, the photo goes into the game's save folder. BACK (Esc) closes the app.
- SELMOTES 😊: a button in the CAMERA app that opens a picker with three faces for ANA or DANIEL: SMILE (a big toothy grin with lifted cheeks), NEUTRAL, and GOOFY FROWN (a huge pout with the lower lip pushed out and worried, scrunched brows). Click one or press 1, 2 or 3. The face stays on while you take the photo, so it appears in the saved picture. It starts on NEUTRAL each time and resets when you leave the camera. In a car the app opens with the rear camera and shows a message that selfies work on foot. The app also works inside the house.
