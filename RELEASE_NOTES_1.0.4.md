ファイアアウト・シャイン FIREOUT SHINE v1.0.4

- NEW PLAYABLE CHARACTER: MIYOYAZU, an adult construction worker who joins ANA and DANIEL. She wears a hi-vis orange vest with reflective strips over a charcoal work tee, khaki cargo work trousers with knee pads, tan work boots and a yellow hard hat. She has the same real five-digit hands and swinging arms as the others.
- The character switch (top left) now shows three high-resolution headshots: ANA, DANIEL and MIYOYAZU, with the same active border. Press Q to cycle through them, or click a headshot to pick that character. Two of the crew are on screen at once. The character you switch to takes over where you were standing, and your partner keeps following you.
- MIYOYAZU starts unarmed like the others. The weapon wheel, the EX IX G9 phone (hers is orange), the CAMERA app with SELMOTES, the house, cars, taxis, races and police chases all work for her.
- MIYOYAZU ability BUILD (B, or the BUILD button): type anything and she builds it in front of her, in the same style as the Tokyo city: real-scale smooth solid buildings with the city's own window, glass curtain wall and shopfront textures (they switch day and night with the rest of the city), painted signs, real roof shapes and normal city trees. No blocks and no glow. Try pagoda (five tiers with smooth curved, upturned roofs and a golden spire), house, apartment, skyscraper, temple, torii, castle, shop or "ramen shop", lighthouse, observation tower, dome, stadium, pyramid, footbridge, fountain, park or "sakura park". She can also build recognisable animal statues (cat, dog, fox, bear, panda, rabbit, pig, cow, horse, lion, tiger, elephant, giraffe and more, with ears, faces, whiskers and tails), vehicles (car, bus, rocket, jet plane, boat), a robot and a figure statue. Misspellings are matched to the closest word ("pyrmid" becomes pyramid). Anything unknown gets its own invented city building with your word on its sign. She hammers away while the structure rises out of the ground. Builds are solid: walls block you, and steps, decks and ramps can be walked on (try "stairs"). UNDO removes the last build and CLEAR ALL removes them all.
- MIYOYAZU ability TSUNAMI (N, the TSUNAMI button, or SUMMON TSUNAMI when she stands on HIKARI BEACH): pick SMALL, MEDIUM or BIG, or set the severity on a slider. A siren sounds and a red 津波警報 TSUNAMI WARNING banner appears. Then a foaming wave rolls in from the sea over HIKARI BEACH and on into Tokyo, with splashes along the front, and the water level rises: knee-deep for SMALL, 2 m for MEDIUM, 4 m and deep into the city for BIG. Cars are pushed inland and float and rock, and pedestrians are swept along. You are never trapped: in deep water everyone floats and treads water with their head above the surface, and you can keep swimming or driving. STOP TSUNAMI (N or the button) drains the water and puts everything back to normal. Only MIYOYAZU can start a tsunami, but anyone can stop it.
- NO MORE GLOW: the over-bright halo on the city, the house and everyone's skin and legs is gone. It came from the bloom post-process filter, plus over-driven daylight in the shader (diffuse light that could push past white, a strong Fresnel/rim term and a pink skin rim light), halo sprites around street lights by day, and over-bright shop windows. Bloom has been removed completely, the daylight is balanced with a soft highlight roll-off so nothing clips to white, the rim light is gone and shop windows are toned down. Neon signs stay readable with no halo.
- CLOTHING in the pause menu (Esc or MENU): change outfits anywhere using the same outfit list and logic as the house wardrobe. The outfit you are wearing is highlighted, and a change applies immediately to the active character and is saved. It works for ANA, DANIEL and MIYOYAZU. In a car you are asked to exit the vehicle first, and it is unavailable in the shower or while swimming.
- NPCs SPEAK JAPANESE: most pedestrians, shop staff, ramen staff, police, race rivals, paramedics, the 119 operator and the drivers you crash into now speak natural Japanese, with the English translation underneath in smaller text, as a speech bubble or a two-line subtitle. A few tourists still speak English only. The game now bundles a full-coverage Japanese font (Noto Sans JP subset, SIL Open Font License) that covers every character used, so there are no missing glyphs. A build-time check scans every line of dialogue against the font and fails the build if any character is missing. Japanese lines wrap correctly: punctuation such as 。、！？」 never starts a new line.
- Pedestrians no longer look like copies of ANA or DANIEL. Women and men now walk the streets with their own clothes, trouser colours, hair colours and hairstyles (fuller, cropped or a bun), with no choker and no floral shirt. They keep the same realistic skin quality with a slight natural tone variation.
- JP TAXI: the windows are now clean see-through tinted glass in a proper black frame (pillars, seals, windscreen and rear-screen surrounds). The torn, peeling yellow edges where the body met the glass are fixed: the body paint is fully opaque and only the glass is transparent, slightly inset so it never z-fights the body. Inside there are seats, a dashboard and door cards. The driver is an adult Indian man with a warm brown skin tone, short dark hair, a thick dark moustache (no beard), a plain white short-sleeved T-shirt and five-finger hands on the wheel. He has no hat, and his back and shoulders are smooth.
- TAXI RIDES: you get in on the back seat in first person, looking at the driver, the dashboard and the city through the windscreen. He turns to you and asks "Hello! Where to today?" in spoken British English, with subtitles and moving lips. The voice is pre-rendered offline with Piper TTS (en_GB northern_english_male voice, trained on the OpenSLR 83 dataset, CC BY-SA 4.0; credited in the game files), so no internet is needed. Pick a destination in first person and the camera glides out to a chase view behind the taxi. Press C to switch between first person and the rear view. On arrival he says "Here we are." and "That'll be ¥X, cheers."
- RECKLESS TAXI DRIVER: he drives about 1.4x faster than normal traffic, weaves and cuts corners, and usually bumps another car once or twice per ride. Your taxi jolts, the other car spins away honking, there are sparks and a camera shake, and its driver shouts at you in Japanese (with English underneath). He doesn't brake at all. He's back at full speed within a second, completely unbothered ("Whoops!", "Anyway...", "Lovely day for it!"), and you still always reach your destination.
- DENSE TRAFFIC: roads are much busier, with about 4x the AI cars on HIGH. Cars are pooled and spawn around you out of view, then despawn far away. New traffic signals at every city junction run a shared red/green cycle: cars stop at the line on red, queue behind each other, turn and honk, and no longer gridlock in junctions. A new TRAFFIC DENSITY setting (LOW / NORMAL / HIGH, default HIGH) is in the quality menu.
- ANIMATED EATING: EAT SNACK in the kitchen is now a 4-5 second animation. The character opens the fridge, takes an onigiri, chips or pocky in their hands, takes bites (the snack shrinks and the mouth chews), then puts the wrapper away. Health is restored as before. It works for all three characters.
- FOOD in the pause menu: buy and eat anywhere, choosing from バーガー BURGER, おにぎり ONIGIRI, 寿司 SUSHI, カップラーメン RAMEN CUP, たこ焼き TAKOYAKI, 餃子 GYOZA, たい焼き TAIYAKI, ポッキー POCKY, メロンパン MELON PAN and 弁当 BENTO, plus 水 WATER and 緑茶 GREEN TEA. Each item has an icon, a price and its own 3D prop, and plays the same animated eating. Water and tea also sober you up after the HIKARI BEACH BAR. It is blocked while driving ("Pull over to eat").
- IX LAPTOPOS G9: a laptop alongside the phone (LAPTOP button in the top bar, or L). Your character takes out the laptop and types, then IX LAPTOPOS G9 boots up with its splash screen, wallpaper, taskbar and clock. It has these apps: BANK, MAP (set a waypoint), SHOP (order outfits or food), MUSIC, EMAIL (messages in Japanese with English underneath), PHOTOS (your selfies), the IX NAVI BROWSER and MISTO 2, plus SHUT DOWN. There is also a laptop on the desk at home ([E] USE LAPTOP). It can't be used while driving.
- IX NAVI BROWSER: an address bar, back, forward and refresh, tabs, and a home page with search, covering a whole in-game web. Sites include Tokyo news (with live TSUNAMI warnings and your police chases), the FRGARAGE car dealer, HONOO RAMEN, a weather site, a real-estate site, a HYPERION DIGITAL EX fan page and the KIRAKIRA social feed of your selfies. Unknown addresses get a friendly 404 page.
- DELETE PHOTOS: every selfie post in the social feed and every photo in PHOTOS has a DELETE button. It asks "Delete this photo? / この写真を削除しますか？", then removes the photo from the feed and the gallery and deletes the PNG from Pictures\FIREOUT SHINE. Only files the game saved are deleted. Deletions persist after a restart.
- MISTO 2: an original disappearing-photo messaging app, on the phone and the laptop. It opens the selfie camera with SELMOTES and lenses (sparkles, cat ears and sunglasses that follow your face, a FIREOUT SHINE frame and a TOKYO frame) and adds captions. Send snaps to friends, who reply with their own snaps. You build a 🔥 streak and can post to STORIES. Saved snaps go to Pictures\FIREOUT SHINE and show up in PHOTOS and the social feed.
- New places: a ramen stall (yatai) in AKARI-CHO with Japanese-speaking staff, and the HIKARI BEACH BAR with an animated drink order.
- Planned for v1.0.5: the bigger "cinematic Tokyo" visual upgrade, with real-time sun shadows, filmic tone mapping and grading, new road, sidewalk and facade materials, more street dressing (poles and wires, vending machines, scramble crossings), a GRAPHICS QUALITY preset and camera polish. It is all designed to stay free of bloom and glow.
